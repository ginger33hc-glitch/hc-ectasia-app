# LASIK planning defaults

Effective LASIK planning sequence for the CER-AI:

1. Plan 1 (default): flap 100 µm; optical zone 6.5 mm; transition zone 9.0 mm.
2. If Plan 1 is not acceptable, Plan 2: flap 100 µm; optical zone 6.0 mm; transition zone 8.5 mm.
3. If Plan 2 is not acceptable, Plan 3: flap 90 µm; optical zone 6.0 mm; transition zone 8.5 mm.

Selection is strictly first-safe: Plan A is selected whenever it is safe. Plan B
is evaluated only after Plan A fails, and Plan C is evaluated only after both
Plan A and Plan B fail. Every displayed selected plan must include its flap,
optical-zone, and transition-zone definition.
4. Every plan is a full CER-AI re-evaluation. No prior plan result is carried forward as a substitute for recalculation.
5. All CER-AI hard stops, tomography/topography requirements, structural calculations, RSB, PTA, refractive gates, and other applicable decision criteria remain active for every plan.
6. Plan 3 is the end of this predefined sequence; no further automatic optimization is defined here.
7. The final surgical decision and responsibility remain with the surgeon.
