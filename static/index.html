An uploaded Excimer Laser Takip Kartı may auto-fill empty sphere, cylinder, and axis fields only from the Düzeltme Miktarı row. Manual values take priority, and uncertain or conflicting readings are not auto-filled. Cylinder is entered as a non-negative magnitude in minus-cylinder convention. MRSE = sphere − cylinder magnitude/2. If actual maximum ablation is blank, an explicitly documented Alcon EX500 plan may use the HC estimate: 12 µm/D at 6.0 mm, 15 µm/D at 6.5 mm, or 16.33 µm/D at 7.0 mm. Other platforms or optical zones require the actual maximum ablation.
