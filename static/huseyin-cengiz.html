Clinical author and reviewer

Hüseyin Cengiz, M.D.

Ophthalmic surgeon and developer of CER-AI — Corneal Ectasia Risk Assessment Intelligence.

Clinical background

Dr. Cengiz has approximately 30 years of clinical and surgical ophthalmology experience. His clinical work has included refractive, cataract and vitreoretinal surgery. He is the founder of Vision Eye Hospital and the clinical lead responsible for CER-AI's public educational content.

Role in CER-AI

Dr. Cengiz defines the clinical questions that CER-AI is intended to organize, reviews the public medical content, and maintains the boundary between image transcription, rule-based assessment, evidence interpretation and surgeon judgment.

CER-AI was developed to make preoperative corneal ectasia risk review more structured, transparent and auditable. Its risk pathways remain separately interpretable; the software does not replace examination, image-quality review or the treating surgeon's decision.

Authorship standard

Public clinical pages identify their sources and evidence limits. Publications supporting an individual variable, scoring system or clinical concept are not presented as external validation of the complete CER-AI product. Material changes are reviewed against the cited literature and the current documented software behavior.

Scope: this profile documents authorship and clinical responsibility for CER-AI's public materials. It is not a patient-care page and does not provide individualized medical advice.
