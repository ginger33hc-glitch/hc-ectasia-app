From HC Ectasia App to CER-AI

The project began as HC Ectasia App, a focused ectasia-screening tool. As it expanded to include multiple risk systems, Pentacam integration, tissue calculations, surgical eligibility, reporting, and planning support, it evolved into CERAI and subsequently the current CER-AI platform.

HC Ectasia App → CERAI → CER-AI

How CER-AI Works

Independent assessment pathways

Randleman / ERSS, Final BAD-D, CER-AI-adapted NICE, PS3, and tissue-safety calculations are evaluated without collapsing them into one unsupported score.

Pentacam source control

Important measurements are linked to defined Pentacam source fields. Missing or conflicting information is identified explicitly rather than silently inferred.

Developer and Clinical Lead

Hüseyin Cengiz, M.D. conceived and developed CER-AI from approximately 30 years of clinical and surgical experience in ophthalmology, including refractive, cataract, and vitreoretinal surgery. He is the founder of Vision Eye Hospital and served as Chief Director and Lead Surgeon.

CER-AI grew from the effort to translate decades of clinical refractive-surgery decision-making into a structured digital framework.

Artificial intelligence should support clinical judgment, not replace it.

CER-AI organizes and cross-checks clinical information, applies predefined safety rules, and documents the reasoning behind an assessment. The final surgical decision always remains with the surgeon.
